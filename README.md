## Our Wonderful Story

1. Once upon a time, an ancient **castle** raised the **Hallowed Flame** on its starward spire— to guide travelers… and hide the land’s oldest secret.  
   ![castle](img/castle.png)

2. That same night, a **dragon** circled the flame. It did not attack; it watched us, as if judging our hearts.  
   ![dragon](img/dragon.png)

3. At dawn, a **detective** arrived with a cracked **map**. A short note read: “Walk as one, or be lost.”  
   ![detective](img/detective.png) ![map](img/map.png)

4. We launched a scout **rocket** first to find a path through the storm. Its trail led us to the only safe mountain pass.  
   ![rocket](img/rocket.png)

5. At the pass, a **knight** asked just one question—“Together?” When we answered “Yes,” he raised the gate.  
   ![knight](img/knight.png)  

6. Beyond the gate, a **wizard** tested our resolve, then placed a glowing **treasure** in our hands.  
   “This is a key of unity. If you use it divided, it will fail.”  
   ![wizard](img/wizard.png) ![treasure](img/treasure_open.png)

7. On the road, a **pirate** tried to seize the key. We kept it safe—and noticed our own faces reflected in the pirate’s eyes.  
   ![pirate](img/pirate.png)

8. A **ghost** lured us off the path, and an **ogre** smashed the bridge. By holding on to one another, we crossed and found the true route again.  
   ![ghost](img/ghost.png) ![ogre](img/ogre.png)

9. At a high cliff, an **alien** rang a silent bell that sounds only for a single will. A sealed door appeared in the rock.  
   ![alien](img/alien.png)

10. In the dark, a **scientist** tuned the key while a **cat** purred in her arms; the lock began to turn.  
    We spoke the dragon’s true name **together**—and the **detective’s** shadow shed like scales, revealing the judge who had disguised itself as our guide.  
    The door opened. Fire no longer burned us; it became a road of light. We walked through as one—  
    and left our old names on the far side of the gate.  
    ![scientist](img/scientist.png) ![cat](img/cat.png)
